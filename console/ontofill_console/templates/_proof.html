Sandbox proof

Two instances. One boundary. The control plane plans and dispatches; every page the agents touch is opened in a throwaway sandbox pod on the sandbox host, never in this app. Each job records six checkpoints. If an agent were told to run rm -rf /, only its pod would be affected, and the pod is deleted when the job ends.
